Refund policy

Last updated:

Short version: if something went wrong with a payment, we fix it or refund you. Gems you haven't used can be refunded within 14 days of buying. Email with your Bachs payment reference.

Gems you haven't used

Within 14 days of a purchase, you can ask for a refund of a gem pack as long as the gems from that pack haven't been used. We remove the gems and refund the full price.

Gems you have used and the supporter pack

When you buy, you agree to immediate delivery and confirm that your right of withdrawal ends once the content is delivered. Gems you have used and an unlocked supporter pack therefore aren't refundable, except as described below or where the law requires.

Always refunded or fixed

  • You paid but the gems or pack never arrived: we deliver them or refund you.
  • You were charged twice or the wrong amount: we refund the difference.
  • A child bought something without a parent's permission: a parent or guardian can contact us and we will look at it fairly and quickly.
  • We close the game: gems bought in the last 14 days that haven't been used are refunded.

How to ask

  1. Email with the email address of your game account and the Bachs payment reference (on your Bachs receipt).
  2. We reply within 5 working days.
  3. Refunds go back through Bachs to the way you paid, usually within 14 days of our approval.

Please contact us before opening a chargeback with your bank. Chargebacks for content that was delivered and used may lead to the gems being removed.
