Privacy policy

Last updated:

Short version: you can play without an account and we receive nothing. If you sign in, we keep your email, your save and your game stats so you can play on any device and appear on the leaderboard. We don't sell data, we don't send marketing emails, and you can download or delete everything from inside the game (👤 Me → Privacy & your data).

We never ask for your real name, address, phone number or birthday. For the age check we keep only your age group, on your device.

Ads

Ads are optional: you only see one when you tap "Watch an ad" (for in-game cash or to recover in hospital). Ads are provided by Google (AdSense H5 Games Ads). If you agree to personalised ads (16+ only), Google may use cookies and device identifiers to choose ads for you; otherwise Google shows non-personalised ads that use only basic information like your rough location. Players who say they are under 16 always get non-personalised ads marked for children. In the EU and UK, Google may also show its own consent message. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), servers in the EU (Ireland).
  • Vercel (hosting the game and, with your consent, visit statistics), USA, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Bachs Technologies Limited (bachs.io, payments), Nigeria. You pay on Bachs's own page; we never see your card, bank or mobile-money details.
  • Google (sign-in with Google if you choose it; ads), Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA.

Fonts and all game art, sounds and music are served from our own site: loading the game sends nothing to font services.

Emails

We only send emails you ask for: the sign-in link. We send no newsletters or marketing. If we ever offer a newsletter, it will be opt-in and every email will have an unsubscribe link.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download, or delete your data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

  • Download your data: 👤 Me → Privacy & your data → Download my data.
  • Delete your account and online data: 👤 Me → Privacy & your data → Delete my account.
  • Delete data on this device: 👤 Me → Privacy & your data → Delete data on this device.
  • Change consent: 👤 Me → Privacy & your data (statistics, personalised ads).
  • Or email . We answer within one month.

You may complain to a data protection authority, for example , the authority where you live, or in Nigeria the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

Children

We ask for your age group when you first open the game. Under 13: no account and no purchases; the game works fully offline on your device. 13–15: an account needs a parent or guardian's permission, and ads are never personalised. Under 18: purchases need a parent or guardian's permission. If you think a child has given us personal data without permission, email and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). The database only lets players read their own data, and money and gems can only be changed by our server.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the game before the change applies.
