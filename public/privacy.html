Playing without an account

You can play without signing in. Your game is then saved only in your browser (local storage) on your device. We do not receive it.

Feedback and visit statistics

If you use the in-game feedback box, we receive your message, its category and star rating, your language, the in-game day and the game version (and your account ID if you are signed in). Please don't put personal details in it.

We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics: which pages are opened, the referring site, country, browser, operating system and device type. It uses no cookies and does not identify you.

What we collect if you sign in

  • Email address, to sign you in (by email link or Google).
  • Your game save, so you can continue on another device.
  • Finished games (character name, trait, ending, days, in-game money earned), shown on the public leaderboard. Use a character name that doesn't identify you.
  • Purchases: a payment reference and amount. We never see your card, bank or mobile-money details; Bachs processes them.
